Enzo · Data Intelligence

Making a data platform impossible to miss.

Enzo needed its trade show presence to say the same thing its product does: confident, modern, and in command of complexity. We designed the booth, signage, and on-site messaging around one line, "Built for Business," and built a visual system, dark, sharp, and unmistakably tech, that held up under show lighting on a crowded floor.

BodyLogicMD · Hormone Therapy & Integrative Medicine

Consistent messaging across dozens of locations.

BodyLogicMD runs a hormone therapy and integrative medicine network built on local trust, which meant every trade show booth and print piece needed to feel like the same brand no matter which practitioner was behind the table. We designed a signage system built around clear offers and a consistent visual identity, so the message stayed sharp whether it was one location or fifteen.

Evexias · Health Solutions

Sell sheets that do the selling.

Evexias needed sales materials that practitioners would actually use in the room, not just leave in a folder. We designed a set of nutraceutical sell sheets that lead with the offer, back it with real numbers, and give the sales team a clean, consistent look across the whole product line.
