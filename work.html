BodyLogicMD · Trade Show & Signage

Consistent messaging across dozens of locations.

BodyLogicMD runs a hormone therapy and integrative medicine network built on local trust, which meant every trade show booth and print piece needed to feel like the same brand no matter which practitioner was behind the table. We designed a booth and signage system built around clear offers and a consistent visual identity, so the message stayed sharp whether it was one location or fifteen.

Beyond the booth, the BodyLogicMD network needed print materials that felt just as consistent: brochures breaking down treatment programs, supplement inserts, patient welcome books, and program booklets for locations across the country.
